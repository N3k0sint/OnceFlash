Privacy Policy & Terms

OnceFlash is built on a strict Zero-Knowledge architecture. Privacy is not a feature we toggle—it is mathematically enforced by end-to-end client-side cryptography.

1. Zero-Knowledge Cryptography

All encryption and decryption happens exclusively inside your web browser via window.crypto.subtle (Web Cryptography API) using AES-256-GCM.

The decryption key is embedded in the URL hash fragment (e.g. #<id>:<key>). In accordance with RFC 3986 §3.5, hash fragments are never transmitted across the network to our servers. As a result, our servers never see, possess, or store your encryption keys or plaintext notes.

2. Ephemeral Storage & Irreversible Purge

Ciphertext blobs are stored temporarily in volatile memory (RAM or Redis) with a strict expiration timer (TTL) set by the sender.

  • Burn-on-Read: Notes configured for 1 view are deleted atomically immediately upon retrieval using atomic server scripts to prevent double-read race conditions.
  • Automatic Expiration: When a note reaches its view limit or expiry duration, it is permanently wiped and unrecoverable.
  • No Backups: Ephemeral notes are never written to permanent disk storage, archives, or backup tapes.

3. Zero Tracking, Cookies, or Analytics

OnceFlash does not monitor your behavior or track you across the internet:

  • No User Accounts: You never register, log in, or provide personal identification.
  • No Cookies: We do not use persistent tracking cookies, marketing pixels, or local tracking beacons.
  • No Third-Party Analytics: No Google Analytics, no Facebook pixels, and no surveillance scripts.
  • No Referrer Leakage: Strict Referrer-Policy: no-referrer headers prevent your secret link fragment from leaking to outbound links.
